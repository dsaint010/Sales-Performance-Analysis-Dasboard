# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard — Excel

## 📊 Project Overview
![Sales Performance Dashboard](Sales-Dashboard.png)

This project analyzes sales performance across products, regions, and salespersons using Microsoft Excel.

The goal was to identify major revenue contributors, compare sales performance, and build an interactive dashboard that supports data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance across products, regions, and salespersons to identify the major contributors to revenue and uncover areas of strong and weak performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel 2019
- Data Cleaning
- Data Validation
- Excel Formulas
- Conditional Formatting
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Dashboard Design
- Data Analysis

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- Analyzed total sales by product, region, and salesperson.
- Compared sales performance across different regions.
- Identified the products generating the highest total sales.
- Compared salespersons based on total sales and quantity sold.
- Analyzed average sales per transaction across regions.
- Used Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to interactively explore the data.
- Created an interactive dashboard to present key sales insights.

## 💡 Key Findings

- Laptop generated the highest total sales among all products.
- Portharcourt recorded the highest regional sales.
- Michael was the top-performing salesperson by total sales.
- Lagos recorded the highest number of transactions.
- Laptop and Portharcourt were major contributors to overall revenue.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

- Maintain strong inventory and availability of Laptop products because they generate the highest sales.
- Investigate the factors driving Portharcourt's strong regional performance and consider applying successful strategies to other regions.
- Recognize and learn from the sales strategies used by Michael, the top-performing salesperson.
- Investigate the high transaction volume in Lagos to identify opportunities for increasing revenue per transaction.
- Monitor lower-performing products and regions and develop targeted strategies to improve their sales performance.
- Continue using the interactive dashboard to monitor sales performance and support data-driven decision-making.

- Key business insights
